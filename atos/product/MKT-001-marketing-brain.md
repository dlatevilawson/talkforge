## Golden rule

> Nobody wakes up thinking, “I need an AI communication simulator.”  
> They wake up thinking, “I hope I don’t mess up today’s presentation.”

---

## Two brains

| Brain | Lives | Job |
|---|---|---|
| **Coach Brain** | Inside the product | Teach listening, cadence, tone, influence |
| **Marketing Brain** | Front door | Make humans stop, click, trust, begin |

---

## 11 non-negotiable rules (summary)

1. Sell the transformation, never the feature  
2. Sell outcomes, not tools  
3. Curiosity beats explanation  
4. Make them see themselves  
5. Bestow an identity  
6. Show the future (pre-live advantage)  
7. Challenge assumptions  
8. Create curiosity gaps  
9. Leverage high stakes & friction  
10. Pass “Why should I care?”  
11. Speak to the conversation — never the technology  

**Blacklist (member-facing marketing/UI):** AI · Machine · Analysis · Simulator · Voice Model · Assessment · Engine  

**Mandatory contexts:** job interview · difficult conversation · salary negotiation · pitch · first date · hard apology · family discussion

## Pre-flight gate

Before shipping any ad, subject line, or UI string:

> Would this make someone stop scrolling at 11:30 PM on a Tuesday?

Must tick at least three: curiosity gap · concrete transformation · high-status identity · emotionally specific language · real-life conversation (not software).

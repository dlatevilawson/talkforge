## Purpose

HBF does **not** explain every aspect of human psychology.

It improves TalkForge’s understanding of the people it exists to serve — transforming observations about human communication into evidence-informed institutional knowledge that improves products, coaching, design, research, and decision-making.

| Optimize for | Do not optimize for |
|---|---|
| Clarity | Completeness |
| Disciplined learning | Certainty |
| Progressively wiser understanding | Psychological encyclopedia |

## Foundational principle

Every observable behavior is an attempt to satisfy one or more human needs within a specific context.

Communication is rarely the goal. Communication is usually the bridge toward something more fundamental.

TalkForge studies communication as a pathway toward human flourishing — not an isolated skill.

---

## Scientific standard (evidence classification)

Every claim identifies one of:

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Established Research** | Well-established behavioral, communication, learning, or cognitive science |
| **Internal Evidence** | Observed repeatedly via TalkForge users, analytics, interviews, or experiments |
| **Working Hypothesis** | Plausible; not yet sufficiently validated |
| **Open Question** | Unanswered; requires investigation |

No statement becomes Canonical solely because it appears persuasive.

**Confidence ladder (aligns KA):** Observation → Hypothesis → Investigated → Verified → Canonical → Superseded → Archived.

Phase I models are predominantly **Working Hypothesis** + **Established Research** scaffolds, with **Internal Evidence** sparse until CE/beta volume grows.

## Human Dignity Requirement

Behavioral understanding shall never be used to manipulate vulnerability.

It exists only to: improve learning · increase preparedness · strengthen agency · protect dignity · improve human outcomes.

---

## Institutional learning requirement

HBF is a living knowledge system. Every meaningful product release, interview, coaching interaction, experiment, and research initiative should be able to improve it.

It shall never become static.

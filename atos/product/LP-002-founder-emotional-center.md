## Verdict

**Find Your Voice.** is the emotional center of the brand.

It invites. It does not explain.  
**Master the Art of Communication** speaks to the mind — retire it from public face.

## Protected lines

| Line | Role |
|------|------|
| **Find Your Voice.** | Headline / emotional center |
| **Every life is shaped by conversations. Practice the ones that matter.** | Wisdom — not marketing |
| **A company, not a product.** | Institutional signal — long-term purpose |

## Section naming

Prefer substance over generic labels.  
**What We Believe** over “What We Stand For” / generic mission chrome.

## Doctrine: leave room for mystery

Homepage job: make people curious enough to take the next step.  
Do not explain everything. Resist text-heavy expansion.  
Refs: Apple · Notion · Linear pacing — not denser decks.

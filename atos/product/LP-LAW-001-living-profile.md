## Living Profile Law #001

> **The Living Profile is the single source of truth for who the member is becoming.**

No feature may introduce identity fields outside the Living Profile without an explicit Founder decision.

---

## Corollaries

1. **Dashboard, Timeline, Life Compass, Progress, Reports, Home, and Coach UI are viewers.** They may not maintain a parallel store of identity, purpose, principles, seasons, milestones, or conversation lifecycle state.

2. **PCM-001** may store communication observations and competency evidence. It does not become a second identity profile. Derived identity-facing claims flow through Understanding → Living Profile with provenance and evidence (POM-001 · Forge Law #014).

3. **Schema expansion of identity** requires Founder decision recorded in `atlas/decisions.md`. Engineering convenience is not sufficient cause.

4. **After System 1 freeze**, new capabilities **consume** the Living Profile. They do not grow it by default.

---

## Writers (exhaustive)

Only two writers may update Living Profile identity:

| Writer | May write |
|---|---|
| Member | Declared fields (goals, principles, seasons, milestones, coaching preferences, confirmations/dismissals) |
| Intelligence Engine | Derived fields — only with provenance, evidence, confidence, and confirmation lifecycle |

Experiences never write identity.  
(Forge Law #016)

---

## Rationale

Without this law, TalkForge becomes five products that each remember a slightly different person. Continuity dies. Trust dies. Schema creep wins.

With this law, every surface asks one question:

> Given who this person is becoming, what is the most helpful thing to do next?

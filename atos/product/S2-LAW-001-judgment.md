## System 2 Law #001

> **Every recommendation must reduce the distance between who the member is today and who they’re trying to become.**

If a recommendation cannot answer that question, it should never appear.

---

## Corollaries

1. **Purpose is mandatory.** Every Mission includes how this step serves who they’re becoming — not only why it is urgent.

2. **Evidence is mandatory.** Every Mission includes why now, with tappable basis (Forge Law #014). Evidence without Purpose is incomplete; Purpose without Evidence is incomplete.

3. **One primary recommendation.** Multiple equal “top” missions violate this law’s spirit — judgment requires choosing.

4. **Hypothesis ≠ assumption.** Discovery may invite practice aligned with season or role; it may not declare needs as facts.

5. **System 1 remains sovereign.** System 2 may not invent identity fields. It may only read Living Profile + Reality and propose next experience.

6. **Relevance over engagement.** Recommendations that increase activity without reducing the distance to becoming fail this law.

---

## Rationale

System 1 became the truth engine.  
System 2 is the judgment engine.

Judgment without identity is a random workout.  
Identity without judgment is a library that never asks you to lift.

This law keeps every homepage, machine, notification, and coach prompt pointed at **one meaningful step forward**.

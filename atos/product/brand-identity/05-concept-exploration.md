## Concept catalog

### Symbolic

#### 01 — Ember Spark
**File:** `01-ember-spark.svg`  
**Why it exists:** Core metaphor — private spark of courage before speech.  
**Communicates:** Ignition, warmth, potential becoming visible.  
**Principles:** Mission alignment · Emotional warmth · Forge without anvil.  
**Weaknesses:** Flame marks are crowded category; camping/energy brand risk.  
**Status:** Advance to review (refine or kill on distinctiveness)

#### 02 — Aperture Light
**File:** `02-aperture-light.svg`  
**Why it exists:** Voice as opening — light through a precise aperture.  
**Communicates:** Focus, clarity emerging from constraint.  
**Principles:** Silence as material · Precision · Scalability.  
**Weaknesses:** Camera/photography brand confusion; cold if over-technical.  
**Status:** Advance (strong craft signal)

#### 03 — Breath Pause
**File:** `03-breath-pause.svg`  
**Why it exists:** The sacred pause before speaking — brand’s emotional truth.  
**Communicates:** Calm, readiness, human breath.  
**Principles:** Forbidden-emotion guard (not hustle) · Human presence.  
**Weaknesses:** May read as Wi‑Fi or sound waves; subtlety can become invisibility.  
**Status:** Conditional — only if refined away from wave cliché

#### 04 — Forge Wedge
**File:** `04-forge-wedge.svg`  
**Why it exists:** Literal craft tool abstracted to geometric wedge.  
**Communicates:** Making, force, form.  
**Principles:** Craft visible · Forge metaphor.  
**Weaknesses:** Aggressive; may feel industrial/weapon-adjacent; weak warmth.  
**Status:** Reject for primary (fails warmth + forbidden macho adjacency)

#### 05 — Shared Flame
**File:** `05-shared-flame.svg`  
**Why it exists:** Two people → one courage; belonging built into form.  
**Communicates:** Connection without handshake cliché.  
**Principles:** Belonging · Mission · Emotional impact.  
**Weaknesses:** Symmetry can feel logo-template; still “flame brand.”  
**Status:** Advance

#### 06 — Voice Vessel
**File:** `06-voice-vessel.svg`  
**Why it exists:** Container that holds then releases voice.  
**Communicates:** Care, holding, emergence.  
**Principles:** Human scale · Warmth.  
**Weaknesses:** Cup/trophy/chalice confusion; anatomical risk if curves go wrong.  
**Status:** Reject (ambiguous object recognition)

#### 07 — Spark Path
**File:** `07-spark-path.svg`  
**Why it exists:** Trajectory from stuck → spoken (practice arc).  
**Communicates:** Motion, progress, arrival of light.  
**Principles:** Transformation · Memorability via story.  
**Weaknesses:** Needs motion to fully land; static may read as comet/logo filler.  
**Status:** Reject as primary (animation-dependent)

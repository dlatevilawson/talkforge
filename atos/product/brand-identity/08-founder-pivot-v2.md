## What was wrong (insight)

We designed around the **company name** (Talk / Forge) and an **implementation metaphor** (forge, flame, spark).

The company is not about fire.  
The company is not about a forge.  
Those are temporary metaphors for practice.

**What deserves a symbol is the mission:**

> Nobody should ever feel voiceless because they don’t know how to express themselves.

---

## New emotional target

| Not the primary emotion | The primary emotion |
|-------------------------|---------------------|
| Power | **Relief** |
| Courage-as-swagger | **Quiet confidence** |
| Prestige / luxury-as-status | **Readiness** |
| Hype hope | **Hope grounded in preparation** |

**User line after practice:**

> “Okay… I think I can do this.”

That feeling shapes every design decision.

Keep:

- Quiet luxury (understated, not status-flex)
- Cinematic
- Human
- Warm
- Timeless

References for *register* (not for copying marks): **Leica · Hermès · Aesop**

Never become:

- Futuristic
- “Tech”
- Playful
- AI-chrome
- Startup-loud

---

## Metaphor kill list (binding)

**Forbidden as primary identity metaphor:**

- Forge / anvil / hammer
- Flame / fire / ember
- Spark / ignition
- Mic / chat bubble / sound wave / brain

---

## Metaphor open list (binding exploration territory)

Symbolize **transformation**, not “communication” (communication is invisible).

Approved territories:

1. A closed shape becoming open  
2. Two separate forms becoming connected  
3. A path unfolding  
4. A doorway  
5. A bridge  
6. A threshold  
7. An opening  

These are timeless. They do not depend on technology or trends.

---

## Symbol requirement

The mark must be recognizable **without reading the word TalkForge**.

Wordmark remains a system layer. It is not the substitute for a symbol.

---

## Process correction before new drawing

Before generating V2 concepts, complete deeper study:

> Why do some of the world’s most enduring identity systems last for decades — principles, not copies.

Questions required (Nike, Apple, Mercedes, Airbnb, and peers): memorability despite simplicity; scale; distance recognition; distinctiveness; why some age and some don’t.

→ See [09-enduring-systems.md](09-enduring-systems.md)

---

## CD commitment

1. Kill forge/flame/spark as identity.  
2. Re-center on mission + relief/readiness.  
3. Study enduring systems deeply.  
4. Explore transformation territories thoroughly (≥30 new distinct concepts).  
5. Do not fall in love with the first V2 solution.  
6. Recommend only if a direction feels like TalkForge — else counsel continued exploration.

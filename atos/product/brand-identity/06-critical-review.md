**Method:** Score finalists on mandatory criteria. Attractiveness alone cannot save a concept.

---

## Criteria definitions

| Criterion | Question |
|-----------|----------|
| **Distinctiveness** | Mistaken for another AI/productivity brand? |
| **Memorability** | Sketchable after one look? |
| **Simplicity** | Survives deletion of detail? |
| **Timelessness** | Alive in 2035 without apology? |
| **Scalability** | 16px and billboard? |
| **Accessibility** | Contrast; meaning without color? |
| **Emotional impact** | Warmth + courage + dignity in <3s? |
| **Mission alignment** | Serves “nobody voiceless…”? |

**Pass threshold for recommendation pool:** average ≥ 3.8 and no criterion ≤ 2.

## Qualitative critique (pool only)

### 01 Ember Spark
- **Strength:** Immediate warmth; forge metaphor without anvil; icon-native.
- **Risk:** Flame category saturation; must be *our* flame through proportion and restraint.
- **Alignment:** Strong with spark→flame→illumination philosophy already in LP doctrine.

### 02 Aperture Light
- **Strength:** Precision and craft; distinctive vs flame cluster; monochrome-clean.
- **Risk:** Reads “camera/optics” before “voice”; emotional warmth is cooler.
- **Alignment:** Clarity and focus yes; belonging and warmth weaker.

### 05 Shared Flame
- **Strength:** Best belonging story in symbolic set; mission-forward.
- **Risk:** Complexity at small sizes; twin-tongue symmetry feels template-prone.
- **Alignment:** Excellent emotionally; craft refinement required.

### 09 Weight Split
- **Strength:** Strategy made visible (Talk soft / Forge strong); highly ownable as verbal identity.
- **Risk:** Weak as solitary app icon; needs companion mark.
- **Alignment:** Best typographic expression of personality.

### 12 Wordmark Geometric
- **Strength:** Name-as-brand discipline; ultimate simplicity.
- **Risk:** Without custom lettering, indistinguishable from SaaS defaults.
- **Alignment:** Clear naming; low emotional narrative alone.

### 27 Orbit Spark
- **Strength:** Silence + spark; sacred smallness; scales; memorable.
- **Risk:** Under-explained; “just a dot on a ring” without brand teaching.
- **Alignment:** Matches cinematic silence doctrine exceptionally well.

### 28 One Stroke Flame
- **Strength:** Earned simplicity; calligraphy of courage; monochrome hero.
- **Risk:** Still in flame family with 01; differentiation is stroke character only.
- **Alignment:** High — if stroke feels human-made, not clipart.

### 35 Horizon
- **Strength:** Full emotional arc (dark→light) in one mark; most distinctive story.
- **Risk:** Favicon compression; flag/tile misread; harder production system.
- **Alignment:** Closest visual thesis to Phase 3 philosophy.

## Review principles applied

1. Did not keep concepts for attractiveness alone.  
2. Penalized category camouflage (connection nodes, hex, sound bars — already rejected in Phase 5).  
3. Required mission + emotion, not only craft.  
4. Accepted that typographic systems may need a companion symbol.  
5. Noted where current SVGs are *directional sketches*, not production finals.

## Pattern study (what kept recurring)

After generating and reviewing all 110:

1. **The most emotionally accurate marks behave like verbs** (Family A, N) — not nouns.  
2. **Paradox marks** (solid + outline, half open / half closed) naturally encode *before and after in one glance* (Family P).  
3. **Negative space** carries relief better than added ornament (Family F).  
4. **Punctuation-of-becoming** (ellipsis→line, ?→.) is surprisingly mission-true and non-tech (Family R).  
5. **Literal human figures and nest/flag/anchor** collapse back into objects — reject.  
6. **Generic geometry** (rings, diamonds, hex) fails the “mistakable for any SaaS” test.  
7. **Chevrons / wedges** drift toward startup arrows — reject.  
8. **V2 object ghosts** (anything door/bridge-readable) must stay dead.

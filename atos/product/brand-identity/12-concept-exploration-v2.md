## Catalog

### Closed → open

#### 01 Circle Opening
**Why:** Simplest “something opens.” Relief as a gap in a closed world.  
**Communicates:** Invitation; incompleteness as hope.  
**Principles:** E1 gesture · E9 relief · mission.  
**Weaknesses:** Open-circle marks are common.  
**Status:** Advance carefully

#### 02 Open Square
**Why:** Structured life with one side released.  
**Communicates:** Order softening into readiness.  
**Principles:** Quiet luxury geometry · transformation.  
**Weaknesses:** May read as unfinished UI frame.  
**Status:** Conditional

#### 03 Capsule Open
**Why:** Two halves of a held form allowing a passage.  
**Communicates:** Held → allowed through.  
**Principles:** Relief · ownable if proportions unique.  
**Weaknesses:** Pill/pharma risk.  
**Status:** Reject (category risk)

#### 04 Lens Relief
**Why:** Opening as focused seeing / being seen.  
**Communicates:** Clarity arriving.  
**Principles:** Opening territory.  
**Weaknesses:** Camera/Leica-adjacent literally; we want register not product confusion.  
**Status:** Reject (camera brand adjacency)

#### 05 Knot Release
**Why:** The stuck feeling loosening — closest to user psychology.  
**Communicates:** Relief from entanglement.  
**Principles:** Emotion = relief · human.  
**Weaknesses:** Hard to simplify; may look like scribble at 16px.  
**Status:** Advance if simplifiable

### Separate → connected

#### 06 Arcs Meet
**Why:** Two paths finding a join point.  
**Communicates:** Connection without handshake cliché.  
**Principles:** Transformation · belonging.  
**Weaknesses:** Wi‑Fi / signal adjacency.  
**Status:** Conditional

#### 07 Join Bars
**Why:** Two uprights become related by a bridge bar — support.  
**Communicates:** Held together; readiness with another.  
**Principles:** H-form craft · scalable.  
**Weaknesses:** Can read as “H” letter or goalpost.  
**Status:** Advance

#### 08 Vesica
**Why:** Overlap as shared space — classic sacred geometry of meeting.  
**Communicates:** Intersection of two lives / inner-outer.  
**Principles:** Timeless geometry · belonging.  
**Weaknesses:** Used by many orgs; mystical overtones.  
**Status:** Advance carefully

#### 09 Becoming Line
**Why:** Separate selves with a path forming between.  
**Communicates:** Connection in progress.  
**Principles:** Story of becoming.  
**Weaknesses:** Generic “nodes + link” tech.  
**Status:** Reject

#### 10 Facing Open
**Why:** Two openings facing each other — dialogue as form, not chat bubble.  
**Communicates:** Mutual presence; listening space.  
**Principles:** Human · relief of being met.  
**Weaknesses:** Parentheses / abstract; may need teaching.  
**Status:** Advance

### Path unfolding

#### 11 Path Unfold
**Why:** Practice as a way that reveals itself step by step.  
**Communicates:** Prepared path; readiness ahead.  
**Principles:** Agency · hope grounded in preparation.  
**Weaknesses:** Staircase/growth-SaaS adjacency.  
**Status:** Conditional

#### 12 Release Spiral
**Why:** Outward release from a tight center.  
**Communicates:** Unwinding tension.  
**Principles:** Relief.  
**Weaknesses:** Can feel techy/hypnotic; spiral brands.  
**Status:** Reject (tech adjacency)

#### 13 Path Gate
**Why:** Journey arriving at a threshold.  
**Communicates:** Approach → enter.  
**Principles:** Dual territory fused.  
**Weaknesses:** Busy at small sizes.  
**Status:** Reject (complexity)

#### 14 Soft Unfold
**Why:** Gentle unfolding curve — exhale.  
**Communicates:** Ease arriving.  
**Principles:** Relief · warm motion.  
**Weaknesses:** Swoosh/telecom generic.  
**Status:** Reject

### Doorway

#### 15 Archway
**Why:** Timeless door — enter readiness.  
**Communicates:** Invitation; sacred ordinary threshold.  
**Principles:** Doorway · quiet luxury · drawable.  
**Weaknesses:** Architecture/religion/portal generics.  
**Status:** Advance

#### 16 Door Ajar
**Why:** The moment of entry — not forced open, slightly open.  
**Communicates:** Permission; “you can come through.”  
**Principles:** Relief · threshold · human.  
**Weaknesses:** Literal door illustration risk; detail at 16px.  
**Status:** Advance (strong story)

#### 17 Portal
**Why:** Frame with warm interior = safety beyond the door.  
**Communicates:** Inside is warmer.  
**Principles:** Quiet luxury color as rare fill.  
**Weaknesses:** App-icon window; depends on color.  
**Status:** Conditional

#### 18 Lintel Gate
**Why:** Monumental threshold; understated structure.  
**Communicates:** Dignity of entry.  
**Principles:** Timeless · architectural calm.  
**Weaknesses:** Torii/shrine cultural collision if too close; severe.  
**Status:** Conditional — soften or kill if cultural risk

### Bridge

#### 19 Arch Bridge
**Why:** Crossing the gap between stuck and spoken.  
**Communicates:** Passage over difficulty.  
**Principles:** Bridge territory · clear metaphor.  
**Weaknesses:** Civic/engineering logo family.  
**Status:** Advance carefully

#### 20 Span
**Why:** Two banks + a joining arc — gap made crossable.  
**Communicates:** “There is a way across.”  
**Principles:** Mission · relief.  
**Weaknesses:** Slightly complex; insurance-company vibe risk.  
**Status:** Advance

#### 21 One Arc Bridge
**Why:** Single gesture bridge — Nike lesson applied to crossing, not motion-sport.  
**Communicates:** One path over.  
**Principles:** E1 gesture · simplicity.  
**Weaknesses:** Smile / McDonald’s-adjacent if curved wrong; swoosh cousins.  
**Status:** Advance with extreme proportion care

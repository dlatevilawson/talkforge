# Phase 7 — Recommendation

**Document ID:** BID-007  
**Authority:** Creative Director recommendation (not Founder Decision until accepted)  
**Date:** 2026-07-27

---

## Executive counsel

After benchmarking world-class systems, defining TalkForge’s emotional job, locking strategy, briefing exploration, generating **35 distinct concepts**, and scoring finalists on meaning — not cosmetics — this is the counsel:

**We have three directions worthy of serious consideration.**  
None is yet a finished world-class mark. All three are **strategic directions** that can become world-class through refinement — not “pick a PNG and ship.”

**I do not recommend locking a final logo file this week.**  
I recommend locking a **direction**, then commissioning craft refinement (custom type + mark geometry) before production replacement of the current LP mark.

That is not indecision. That is refusing to fall in love with the first solution — and refusing to ship a sketch as identity.

---

## Top three concepts

### Finalist A — Horizon (Concept 35)

**Direction:** Hybrid symbolic — silence/clarity split with spark at the threshold.

#### Why it deserves consideration
- Closest visual embodiment of Phase 3 philosophy: *forge at night that becomes morning*.
- Highest distinctiveness score in the pool — does not look like default AI SaaS.
- Emotional arc is readable: dark → spark → light maps to freeze → courage → voice.
- Supports cinematic LP hero (black silence → light) without copying another brand’s mark.

#### Risks
- Small-size legibility: split field can muddle at 16px; needs a simplified favicon sibling.
- Misread as flag, toggle, or generic “dark mode” tile if craft is lazy.
- More complex design system (when to use full horizon vs spark-only).

#### Long-term implications
- Strong brand world: marketing, motion (horizon line revealing), photography grade.
- Forces discipline: product UI must respect darkness/warmth language.
- Ownable story asset for 10+ years if geometry is refined uniquely.

#### CD recommendation on A
**Primary direction for the mark system** — if Founder wants the identity to *feel like transformation*, not just name a product.

---

### Finalist B — Orbit Spark (Concept 27) + optional One-Stroke Flame (28) as motion cousin

**Direction:** Minimal — sacred spark in quiet orbit.

#### Why it deserves consideration
- Best match to “silence is a material” and the sacred-mark LP doctrine.
- Highest simplicity + strong emotional quiet confidence.
- Scales ruthlessly; monochrome-native; app-icon natural.
- Avoids chat/mic/brain clichés completely.

#### Risks
- Under-explained without brand teaching — a ring and a dot need context once.
- Orbit motif exists in other categories (space, loading, planets) — proportion and position must be uniquely TalkForge.
- Less “forge” literalism — name carries forge; mark carries spark/silence.

#### Long-term implications
- Extremely durable system if the spark position/weight becomes signature.
- Pairs cleanly with a custom wordmark (see Finalist C).
- Motion language is elegant: spark travels the orbit once → settles (practice → ready).

#### CD recommendation on B
**Strongest minimal primary mark** — especially paired with typographic Finalist C.  
If Horizon feels too narrative/complex for product chrome, Orbit Spark is the disciplined choice.

---

### Finalist C — Weight Split Wordmark (Concept 09) with companion mark

**Direction:** Typographic system — *Talk* light / *Forge* bold (or soft/strong modulation).

#### Why it deserves consideration
- Makes brand personality visible in the name itself — strategy you can read.
- Highest mission alignment among type directions: speech (Talk) + practice craft (Forge).
- Differentiates from Inter-era SaaS wordmarks when custom-drawn.
- Survives even if symbol trends change — name remains.

#### Risks
- Not sufficient alone as app icon — **requires** companion mark (A or B).
- Poor execution = default stacked type, zero magic.
- Localization / long-name layouts need lockup rules early.

#### Long-term implications
- Builds verbal-visual equity like quality wordmark brands.
- Editorial and story surfaces get immediate hierarchy.
- Becomes the spine of the identity system; symbol is the heart, wordmark is the handshake.

#### CD recommendation on C
**Mandatory system layer** regardless of which symbol wins.  
Do not ship a symbol without a crafted TalkForge wordmark. Do not ship a generic geometric all-caps wordmark and call it done.

### 24 Slit Threshold
Most cinematic. Solid presence with a single way through — very “okay, there’s a way.” Quiet luxury possible. Risk: brutalism / severity vs warmth; must soften corners or material feel in refinement.

### 21 One Arc Bridge

### 20 Span

### 16 Door Ajar

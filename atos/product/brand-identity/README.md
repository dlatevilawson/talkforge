## Standing rules

- Never copy another company’s logo.  
- Never chase trends without strategy.  
- Never optimize aesthetics over meaning.  
- Simplicity is earned through refinement.  
- Explore thoroughly before converging.  
- Reject your own work when it fails the mission.
- **V2:** Mission over name. Transformation over communication. Relief over swagger. No forge/flame/spark.

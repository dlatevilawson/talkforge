## System 2 Law #002

> **The lesson should remain recognizable. The experience should remain surprising.**

The coaching objective may repeat until growth occurs.  
The teaching modality must not become a predictable loop.

---

## Corollaries

1. **What ≠ How.** Readiness owns the objective. Pedagogy owns delivery. Neither may silently do the other’s job.

2. **Repetition of principle is required for growth.** Changing the lesson daily for novelty is a failure mode.

3. **Repetition of format is a failure mode.** Delivering the same objective the same way because it is easier to implement is forbidden.

4. **Variety is intentional.** Modality rotates based on recent modalities used, energy, learning preferences, season, and proximity to real-world conversations — never random shuffle.

5. **Practice over education when stakes are near.** If an important real conversation is imminent, prefer simulation/challenge over passive insight.

6. **Fatigue prefers brevity.** When the member appears depleted, prefer short insight or reflection over intensive drill.

7. **Ask the coach’s question:** *Given today’s coaching objective, what is the most effective way to teach this lesson today?*

---

## Long-term feel

Members should not say: “The coach keeps giving me new content.”  
They should say: “The coach always finds a new way to help me understand the same lesson.”

The coach should never become predictable.  
**The growth should.**

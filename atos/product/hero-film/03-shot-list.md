## How to read this list

Each shot has:

- **ID** — stable reference (used in Prompt Bible)  
- **Duration** — target on timeline (not generation length)  
- **Picture** — what we see  
- **Camera** — movement / lens feel  
- **Emotion** — what the audience should feel  
- **Audio note** — music/silence intent  
- **Cut to** — editorial intention  

Generation platforms may produce 4–12s clips; **Edit Plan** trims to these durations.

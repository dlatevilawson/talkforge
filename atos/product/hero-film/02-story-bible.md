# 02 — Story Bible

**Document ID:** FILM-SB-001  
**Depends on:** [01-creative-brief.md](01-creative-brief.md)  
**Status:** Binding character & emotion source for all shots and prompts  
**Version:** 1.0.0

---

## Purpose

Answer: **Who are these people? What do they feel? Why should we care?**

If a shot or prompt cannot point back to this bible, it does not belong in the film.

---

## World

Contemporary, realistic, warm.  
Cities and homes that feel lived-in — not aspirational sets.  
No near-future tech aesthetics.  
TalkForge exists in this world as a quiet tool, like a notebook or a practice room — not as spectacle.

**Time of day language**

| Act | Light |
|-----|-------|
| Missed moments | Daylight — clear, exposing |
| Night discovery | Warm practical lamps — intimate |
| Practice | Dawn / soft morning arriving — hope beginning |
| Resolution | Golden, earned — not triumphant glare |
| Final hope | Soft window light — hold |

---

## Central truth

These people are not broken.  
They are **unpracticed**.

They already know what they want to say.  
Fear interrupts the moment.  
Practice returns them to life ready.

---

## Characters

### Maya — The Professional (Missed Opportunity → Earned Voice)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Age** | Late 20s |
| **Role** | Young professional in a modern workplace |
| **Want** | To contribute the idea she already has |
| **Wound** | Hesitation costs her recognition |
| **Fear** | Being wrong / speaking out of turn |
| **Private truth** | She knew. She almost said it. |
| **Arc** | Silence in the room → practice → speaks with earned calm |
| **Resolution image** | She presents. The room listens. No swagger — readiness. |
| **Casting note** | Specific face, warm presence, understated power. Avoid “stock confident executive.” |

**Emotional beats for Maya**

1. Breath in — almost speaks  
2. Soft collapse — someone else takes the air  
3. Quiet aftermath — watching another receive what was hers  
4. Night: searching without drama  
5. Practice: imperfect reps, posture changing  
6. Return: voice lands; relief more than triumph  

---

### Jordan — The Partner (Love → Spoken Commitment)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Age** | Late 20s |
| **Role** | Adult child at a family dinner |
| **Want** | To tell his family he plans to marry the woman he loves |
| **Wound** | Love stuck behind fear of reaction / timing / vulnerability |
| **Fear** | Making it real; disappointing; being too much |
| **Private truth** | The words are ready. The courage isn’t — yet. |
| **Arc** | Stops himself → practice → speaks → embrace |
| **Resolution image** | Family smiles. They hold him. Soft, human joy. |
| **Casting note** | Warm, grounded. Disappointment must be visible without melodrama. |

**Emotional beats for Jordan**

1. Glance at parents — decision forming  
2. Mouth opens — then closes  
3. Subject changes — private shame of the dodge  
4. Night: alone with the unsaid  
5. Practice: saying her name out loud to an empty room  
6. Return: the sentence lands; belonging expands  

---

### Ava — The Newcomer (Belonging → Connection)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Age** | Young adult (~20) — **not a minor** |
| **Role** | University / early-adulthood student entering a social world |
| **Want** | To introduce herself; to belong |
| **Wound** | Courage fails at the threshold of other people |
| **Fear** | Rejection; awkwardness; invisibility becoming permanent |
| **Private truth** | She is ready for friendship — practice is the missing step |
| **Arc** | Walks past → practice → introduces herself → walks with someone |
| **Resolution image** | Another student smiles. They leave together. Simple. Huge. |
| **Casting note** | Open face. Courage small and real — never teen-drama tropes. |

**Emotional beats for Ava**

1. Sees laughter she wants to join  
2. Steps toward — then away  
3. Continues alone — the cost of silence  
4. Night: the ache of almost  
5. Practice: introductions in a quiet room  
6. Return: “Hi, I’m Ava.” A smile. Walking together  

---

### The Fourth Face — Hope Received (Finale)

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Role** | Someone new who has just discovered hope |
| **Function** | Proof the gift continues — TalkForge is shared, not hoarded |
| **Emotion** | Quiet relief; almost-smile; eyes that say *maybe I can* |
| **Rule** | Hold the shot. Do not explain. |

Maya, Jordan, or Ava may recommend TalkForge to this person — without a pitch. A look. A few quiet words. A link. Human scale.

## Ensemble emotional map

| Act | Collective feeling |
|-----|-------------------|
| Opening triad | Recognition of the freeze |
| Night | Shared loneliness that is not empty — it is searching |
| Practice | Agency returning |
| Resolution | Life answering practice |
| Finale | Hope as something that spreads |

---

## What each character must never become

| Trap | Why forbidden |
|------|----------------|
| Broken / diagnosed | Violates dignity |
| Comic awkward stereotype | Cheapens fear |
| Instantly transformed by AI | Lies about practice |
| Heroic swagger after success | Wrong emotion — we want relief |
| Product spokesperson | Film is not an ad read |

---

## Dialogue policy

**No spoken dialogue** in the film’s emotional core.  
Faces carry the story.  
If words appear, they are sparse, natural, and never sales language.

Title card only at end: **Find Your Voice. / TalkForge**

---

## Continuity rules (for generation & edit)

1. Same three people from freeze → practice → return.  
2. Wardrobe continuity within each life (subtle progression allowed: night clothes → day clothes).  
3. Environments must feel like the same person’s world.  
4. Practice never looks more cinematic than life — life is the climax.  
5. Product screens stay secondary; eyes and breath stay primary.

---

## Theme line for every scene check

Before locking a shot, ask:

> Does this moment serve *becoming ready* — or only illustrating “communication”?

If the latter, cut it.

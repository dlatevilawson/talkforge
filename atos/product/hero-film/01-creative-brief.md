**Version:** 2.0.0 (system formalization)  
**Status:** **LOCKED for Promo V1** (Founder 2026-07-28) — recreate before app launch  

**Depends on:** Founder assignment · Mission · BID-018 (official mark)

---

## What are we trying to make?

A **30–45 second cinematic brand film** for the TalkForge landing page hero.

Not a product demo.  
Not an AI commercial.  
Not a feature tour.

A premium **human transformation film** — the kind of emotional storytelling associated with the best Apple and Nike brand films (mood and craft only; never copy).

When it ends, the viewer should feel **hope** — not because they saw impressive technology, but because **they saw themselves**.

---

## One-sentence intent

Show the universal moment of becoming ready to say what matters — then invite the viewer into TalkForge without selling software.

---

## Theme (locked)

Every life is shaped by conversations.  
Some people already know what they want to say.  
They simply need a place to practice.  
TalkForge exists to help people find their voice.

---

## Audience

| Primary | Adults who freeze before conversations that matter — work, love, belonging |
| Secondary | People who love someone who freezes — and want to help without replacing their voice |

**Viewer position:** Recognition first. Aspiration second. Product last.

---

## Emotional target

| Must leave with | Must never leave with |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Hope | Hype |
| Relief / readiness | Fear-mongering |
| “That was me.” | “Cool AI.” |
| Quiet confidence | Pity |
| Desire to practice | Desire for a gimmick |

**User line the film earns:**  
*“Okay… I think I can do this.”*

---

## Story spine (high level)

1. Three lives. Three missed moments.  
2. The same private ache at night.  
3. Discovery of a calm place to practice.  
4. Practice — gradual, human, imperfect.  
5. Return to life — earned confidence.  
6. Hope passed to someone new.  
7. Title: **Find Your Voice. · TalkForge**

Full character and emotion detail → [Story Bible](02-story-bible.md).

---

## What the product may do on screen

- Appear **naturally** during search / late-night discovery  
- Feel calm, trustworthy, human  
- Support the journey — **never become the hero**  
- Minimal UI only — no holograms, no magic, no futuristic chrome  

---

## Cinematography (locked)

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Cinematic realism | Startup gloss |
| Natural lighting | Neon / purple AI glow |
| Warm blacks, soft gold highlights | Cold clinical blue |
| Slow, deliberate camera | Whip pans, hype cuts |
| Close-ups that capture emotion | Product screen as hero |
| Shallow depth of field | Busy multi-subject frames |
| Realistic environments | Abstract CGI worlds |
| Understated performance | Exaggerated acting |

**Mood references (craft only — never imitate frames):** Denis Villeneuve pacing · Roger Deakins natural light · Emmanuel Lubezki immersive movement · premium Apple/Nike brand-film restraint.

---

## Music (locked intent)

- Begin: quiet piano  
- Build: subtle strings  
- Allow silence where emotion is louder than score  
- Landing page may play **muted**; master should still be scored for brand use  

Detail → [Edit Plan](05-edit-plan.md).

---

## Brand constraints

- Official mark (Decision 032) may appear only in end title / lockup — not as a floating UI sticker mid-film  
- No forge / flame / spark iconography  
- No mic / chat bubble / sound-wave clichés  
- No depiction of minors (belonging character = young adult)  

---

## Success criteria

The film succeeds if:

1. A stranger feels recognized before they understand TalkForge.  
2. Technology never steals the emotional center.  
3. The ending feels inevitable, not sold.  
4. It still works muted.  
5. It would not be mistaken for a generic AI startup ad.

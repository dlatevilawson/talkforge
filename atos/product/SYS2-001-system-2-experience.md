## How to use this document

**Read SYS1-001 first.** System 1 is truth. System 2 is experience.

System 1 is frozen because we have a trustworthy foundation.  
System 2’s responsibility is no longer to remember — **it is to decide**.

If a proposal conflicts with SYS1-001, SYS1 wins.  
If a proposal conflicts with this document, the proposal loses — unless the Founder amends System 2 explicitly.

## Founding sentence

> **Meet the member where they are, and help them take one meaningful step forward.**

Not ten. **One.**

System 1 founding sentence (still binding):

> TalkForge does not store information so it can remember the past. It organizes information so it can coach the future.

---

## The split (keep it hard)

| | System 1 | System 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Role | **Truth engine** | **Judgment engine** |
| Asks | What do we know? | Given everything we know, what is the **single most helpful** thing to do next? |
| Builds | Brain / Living Profile | Nervous system / attention |
| Must never | Invent identity | Assume identity or invent who they are |
| May | Record, confirm, evidence | Hypothesize, recommend, stage experience |

System 1 answered: who is this person, what matters, how do we remember correctly, how do we earn trust.

System 2 asks: **Now that we understand this person… what should they experience next?**

That is when TalkForge stops looking like software and starts behaving like a coach.

# 1. Mission

> **Deliver the right conversation, at the right time, in the right way.**

Not the most conversations.  
Not every conversation.  

**The next conversation.**

---

# 2. The moat

Not AI.  
Not voice.  
Not memory.  
Not scoring.

**Relevance.**

The app knows the season.  
The app knows the avoided conversation.  
The app knows strengths and growth edge.  
The app knows coaching style and intensity.

So it quietly changes itself — and always shows **why**.

People will not remember elegant schema.  
They will remember:

> “I opened TalkForge after putting off a difficult conversation… and somehow it knew exactly what I needed to practice.”

Brand outcome we optimize for:

> “It’s like having a coach that always knows what conversation you need to practice next.”

# 3. Judgment Law (S2-LAW-001)

> **Every recommendation must reduce the distance between who the member is today and who they’re trying to become.**

If a recommendation cannot answer that question, it must never appear.

Full text: `atos/product/S2-LAW-001-judgment.md`

---

# 4. Hypothesize, never assume

System 2 must **never invent identity** (SYS1 / LP-LAW-001).

Coaching is different from recording.

A great coach does not say:

> “You need to work on delegation.” *(assumption / fact)*

A great coach says:

> “Many first-time managers struggle with delegation. Would you like to practice that?” *(hypothesis / invitation)*

**Doctrine:**

> System 2 may hypothesize. It may never assume.

Every hypothesis is clearly framed as an **invitation**, not a fact.  
Discovery creates delight without sacrificing trust.

---

# 5. Evidence + Purpose (why now + why it matters)

Every recommendation needs both:

| Field | Answers |
|---|---|
| **Evidence** | Why now? |
| **Purpose** | Why does this matter for who I’m becoming? |

Evidence alone is efficient.  
Evidence + Purpose is memorable.

Example:

**Evidence:** You’ve postponed your salary conversation twice, and your review is tomorrow.  
**Purpose:** Practicing this moves you one step closer to becoming the leader you told us you want to be.

Evidence without Purpose is a reminder app.  
Purpose without Evidence is flattery.  
TalkForge needs both.

Provenance still applies (Forge Law #014): decisions must be explainable; identity claims remain evidence-gated.

---

# 6. Optimize for relevance, not activity

> **System 2 exists to reduce decision fatigue, not increase engagement.**

If the homepage has ten recommendations, System 2 has failed.  
If it confidently says *“This is the one conversation that matters most today,”* it has succeeded.

Hard UI rule:

- **One primary mission above the fold**
- Everything else secondary or hidden
- Decline (“Not today”) is data — not failure

---

# 7. Homepage = personal trainer

A trainer doesn’t point at every machine.  
They walk over and say: **“Today we’re working on this.”**

That emotional feeling defines TalkForge.

Examples of System 2 behavior:

| Member | Wrong homepage | Right homepage |
|---|---|---|
| Avoided raise for 3 months | Public Speaking · Small Talk · Storytelling | **Today’s mission: Practice asking for the raise you’ve been postponing** |
| New father | Generic gym floor | Parenting / family / listening as first machines |
| New manager | Unchanged layout | Feedback · Delegation · Performance reviews first |

Same app. Different nervous system response.  
When the gym rearranges, show a thin **why** — relevance without explanation feels creepy and violates System 1 trust.

# 8. Five pillars (responsibilities)

Adaptive Experience must **not** own judgment. It renders what Readiness + Pedagogy decide.

| Pillar | Decides | Must not |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Readiness Engine** | *What* to practice — stable coaching **objective** until growth | Invent identity; flip the lesson daily for novelty |
| **2. Pedagogy Engine** | *How* today’s lesson is delivered — teaching modality | Change the objective just to feel fresh; random variety |
| **3. Conversation Engine** | *How interaction unfolds* inside a practice/sim (interrupt, push back, support, emotion) — reads Coaching Intensity | Rewrite Living Profile identity |
| **4. Reflection Engine** | *What sticks* — ask before score: What changed? What surprised you? What will you do now? | Force journaling; block exit forever |
| **5. Adaptive Experience** | *Skin* — homepage, machines visible, layout | Become a second brain that disagrees with Readiness/Pedagogy |

Flow:

```
Living Profile (System 1)
        ↓
Readiness Engine  →  Coaching Objective (stable)
        ↓
Pedagogy Engine   →  Delivery modality (varies intentionally)
        ↓
Mission (one) = Objective + Modality + Evidence + Purpose
        ↓
Adaptive Experience (renders)
        ↓
Conversation Engine (when modality requires practice)
        ↓
Reflection Engine (meaning + transfer)
        ↓
Understanding → Living Profile updates (evidence-gated)
```

# 8a. Separate the What from the How

This is coaching philosophy — not UI chrome.

### Level One — What (coaching objective)

Decided by **Readiness** from evidence and Living Profile.

Examples: Boundary setting · Negotiation · Executive presence · Active listening · The postponed raise conversation.

**The principle remains constant until meaningful growth occurs.**

Growth requires repetition. System 2 must not change the lesson merely because yesterday it taught the same objective.

Parents teach honesty repeatedly.  
Teachers reinforce fundamentals repeatedly.  
Athletic coaches drill the same skill repeatedly.

The lesson stays.

### Level Two — How (teaching modality)

Decided by **Pedagogy**.

Possible delivery methods include:

- Story  
- Simulation / roleplay  
- Question  
- Reflection  
- Challenge / real-world assignment  
- Scientific insight  
- Historical example / perspective  
- Conversation with Coach Forge  
- Mini lesson  
- Fun fact  
- Journal prompt  
- Audio exercise  
- Visual explanation  

**The coaching objective remains constant. The delivery rotates intelligently.**

Example week — same objective (*become more comfortable setting healthy boundaries*):

| Day | Modality | Experience |
|---|---|---|
| Mon | Story | Narrative of someone setting a difficult boundary |
| Tue | Reflection | “What boundary have you been avoiding this week?” |
| Wed | Simulation | Roleplay saying “no” to an unreasonable request |
| Thu | Science | Why the brain treats conflict as threat |
| Fri | Challenge | Set one small boundary today |
| Sat | Debrief | What happened when you followed through? |
| Sun | Perspective | Quote or historical example on courage and self-respect |

Every day teaches the same principle.  
Every day feels different.

### Founder principle (binding)

> **The lesson should remain recognizable. The experience should remain surprising.**

The coach should never become predictable.  
**The growth should.**

Members should not feel:

> “The coach keeps giving me new content.”

They should feel:

> “The coach always finds a new way to help me understand the same lesson.”

That is how great mentors, parents, athletic coaches, and teachers operate.

# 8b. Pedagogy Engine (Responsibility #5)

**Mission:** Determine how today’s coaching objective should be delivered.

Ask:

> Given today’s coaching objective, what is the most effective way to teach this lesson **today**?

Not: “What content should I show?”  
But: “What **teaching method** will help this person most today?”

**Responsibilities:**

- Select the most appropriate teaching modality  
- Balance repetition of principle with variety of experience  
- Prevent coaching fatigue  
- Adapt to energy, progress, learning preferences, Coaching Intensity, and current season  
- Prefer practice over education when a real-world conversation is imminent  
- Prefer short insight over intensive drill when the member appears fatigued  
- Prefer simulation after a run of stories; prefer reflection after a run of simulations  
- Ensure principles stay consistent while experiences stay fresh  

**Adaptive variety is intentional — never random.**

Variety optimizes for engagement through **meaningful** freshness, not novelty for its own sake.

**Anti-pattern:** Repeating the same experience because it is easier to implement.

# 9. Mission object (recommendation model)

Every primary recommendation is a **Mission** with:

| Field | Required | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Objective** | Yes | Stable *what* — the principle / skill / conversation |
| **Modality** | Yes | Today’s *how* — teaching method (Pedagogy) |
| Conversation / focus | Yes | Concrete practice target when applicable |
| Why now | Yes | Situational urgency |
| Evidence | Yes | Observable basis (tappable) |
| **Purpose** | Yes | Link to who they’re becoming |
| Intensity | Yes | From Coaching Intensity + context |
| CTA | Yes | Begin / Not today / Later |
| Hypothesis? | If discovery | Explicit invitation framing |

Without **Purpose**, recommendations become efficient.  
With **Purpose**, they become memorable.  
Without **Modality**, the gym becomes a repetitive loop.  
With intelligent **Modality**, repetition becomes growth.

# 10. Priority stack (when signals compete)

When Season, Avoidance, and Milestones conflict, rank:

1. **Time-sensitive typed milestone** (e.g. interview tomorrow)  
2. **High-importance conversation** ready to move in the lifecycle  
3. **Season default gym** (primary, then secondary)  
4. **Discovery hypothesis** (invitation only)  
5. Gentle exploration  

Cooldownuration / cooldown: if the member declines the same mission twice, stop leading with it until context changes.

---

# 11. Discovery (first-class)

System 2 shouldn’t only respond. It should occasionally explore.

Pattern:

- Never claim.  
- Offer.  
- Tie lightly to season or role when evidence is thin.  

Examples:

> “You’ve been leading meetings lately. Would you like to practice giving difficult feedback?”

> “Many new parents find boundary-setting becomes harder. Want to rehearse saying no?”

Discovery is hypothesis. Confirmation (or decline) feeds System 1 honestly.

---

# 12. Reflection before score

After a session, Forge does not immediately score.

It asks (invite, skippable):

- What changed?  
- What surprised you?  
- What will you do now?  

That’s where growth becomes transfer.  
Forced journaling kills the gym. Allow skip. Capture when offered.

Transfer loop: “When will you have this conversation?” → updates Conversation lifecycle in Living Profile via Understanding.

---

# 13. Design principles (System 2)

1. One meaningful step — not a menu of ambition.  
2. Personal trainer energy — point to one machine.  
3. Explain relevance — thin “because…” always.  
4. Reduce decision fatigue — never optimize for clicks.  
5. Hypothesis language — invitations over diagnoses.  
6. Purpose + Evidence on every Mission.  
7. Decline is signal.  
8. Experiences still never write identity (Forge Law #016).  
9. **Lesson recognizable; experience surprising** — stable objective, rotating modality.  
10. **Variety is intentional** — never random novelty; never lazy repetition of the same format.

---

# 14. Final System 2 test

Every experience decision must answer:

> **Does this help the member take one meaningful step toward who they’re becoming — right now?**

And:

> **Does this reduce decision fatigue?**

And:

> **Given today’s objective, is this the most effective way to teach the lesson today?**

If any answer is no — don’t ship it.

---

## What System 2 is not

- A content catalog ranked by novelty  
- An engagement maximizer  
- A second Living Profile  
- A place that invents fears, goals, or seasons  
- A homepage with ten equal CTAs  
- A coach that changes the lesson every day to feel “fresh”  
- A coach that repeats the same format because it is easier to build

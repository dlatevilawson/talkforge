## Purpose

Organize communication around **meaningful human moments**, not generic skills. Context is where needs and frictions become concrete.

---

## Context card template

For each context under investigation:

1. Definition of the moment class  
2. Dominant needs  
3. Dominant frictions  
4. Decision pressures (HBF-004)  
5. Success outcomes that matter (HBF-006)  
6. Evidence class + open questions

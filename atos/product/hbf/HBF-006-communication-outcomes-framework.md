## Purpose

Define success beyond performance theater. TalkForge wins when people are more prepared for real moments — with dignity intact.

---

## Outcome dimensions (investigate)

| Outcome | Working definition | Why it matters | Evidence level |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Preparedness** | Felt and demonstrated readiness for a specific upcoming moment | Core product promise | WH · Internal emerging |
| **Clarity** | Ability to express what matters understandably | Communication quality | WH · Established (clarity research) |
| **Agency** | Sense of choice and ownership in the conversation | Autonomy need; AMD | WH · AMD binding |
| **Confidence** | Felt readiness (≠ competence) | Engagement; must not mislead | WH |
| **Listening** | Capacity to receive the other person | Relationship quality | WH · Established |
| **Relationship quality** | Bond/trust trajectory with the other | Connection need | WH |
| **Trust** | Credibility and safety in the interaction | Respect/dignity | WH · AMD |
| **Behavioral transfer** | Use of practiced moves in real life | FLA north star | Established (transfer) · Internal gated by PPS |
| **Long-term retention** | Skills/beliefs that persist beyond the session | Learning integrity | Established · WH (product) |
| **Real-world application** | Concrete use in the user’s stated context | Purpose tether | WH · Internal |

Additional outcomes may emerge through research — add only via KA (question → evidence → review).

## Anti-metrics (do not optimize alone)

| Anti-metric | Risk |
|---|---|
| Words per minute / fluency alone | Performance theater |

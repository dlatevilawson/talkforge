## Purpose

Investigate how communication decisions emerge — so practice can intervene at the right stage (perception, emotion, decision) rather than only scoring surface behavior.

---

## Working sequence

```
Situation
    → Perception
    → Emotion
    → Decision
    → Behavior
    → Outcome
    → Reflection
    → Identity
    → Future behavior
```

**Status of sequence:** **Working Hypothesis** until supported through Internal Evidence and research synthesis. Do not treat as Canonical law.

| Stage | Working meaning | Ties to |
|---|---|---|
| Situation | External moment / context (HBF-003) | ELM Moment |
| Perception | What the person notices / frames | Needs salience (HBF-001) |
| Emotion | Affective state / friction (HBF-002) | ELM Emotion |
| Decision | Choice under constraint | Autonomy / agency |
| Behavior | What is said / not said | Observable practice |
| Outcome | Immediate interpersonal result | HBF-006 |
| Reflection | Sense-making after | FLA learning loop |
| Identity | Updated self-belief | HBF-005 |
| Future behavior | Transfer propensity | FLA transfer / PCM growth |

---

## Design implications (Working Hypothesis)

1. Coaching that only corrects **Behavior** misses Perception and Emotion.  
2. Dignity-safe practice targets **Decision** and **Reflection** without identity attack.  
3. Product “success” must include Outcome + Reflection quality, not fluency alone.

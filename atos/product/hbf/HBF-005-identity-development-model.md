## Purpose

Understand communication practice as **identity development**, not skill accumulation alone — while remaining humble: no conclusions become Canonical without evidence.

---

## Investigation agenda (not conclusions)

| Theme | Working question | Provisional stance | Evidence level |
|---|---|---|---|
| Practice → beliefs | How does repeated practice change beliefs about “who I am in hard conversations”? | Beliefs shift after *interpreted* success, not raw reps | Working Hypothesis |
| Beliefs → behavior | How do beliefs influence approach/avoidance? | Self-efficacy and identity-as-communicator predict engagement | Established Research (general) · WH (product) |
| Success → confidence | How does repeated success build confidence? | Mastery experiences build efficacy; false wins may inflate | Established Research · WH |
| Confidence ≠ competence | How do they differ? | Confidence is felt readiness; competence is demonstrated capability — both matter; conflating harms dignity | Working Hypothesis + AMD caution |
| Identity via deliberate practice | How does identity evolve through deliberate practice? | Identity consolidates when practice is purpose-tethered and reflected (HBF-004) | Working Hypothesis |

---

## Dignity constraints

- Never tell a user who they “are” from thin data.  
- Never pathologize.  
- Prefer: “You’re building capacity for moments that matter.”  
- Confidence-building must not become empty praise or manipulation (AMD-001).

---

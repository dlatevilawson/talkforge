## How to use this document

This Constitution defines what should **rarely change**.

If a proposal conflicts with this Constitution, the proposal loses — unless the Founder amends the Constitution explicitly.

# Constitutional Law #001

> **Nothing that defines TalkForge is Canonical until it has been deliberately admitted by Founder decision.**

Working Knowledge may prepare, propose, and pressure-test.
It does not admit.

Capture in the Idea Vault is not admission.  
Drafting by an agent is not admission.  
Shipping a feature is not admission.  
Informal consensus is not admission.

Only explicit Founder decision admits Canonical Knowledge.

---

# Constitutional Admission Rule

A statement may be **admitted** to this Constitution — or to any other Canonical home — only when all of the following are true:

1. **It defines the company** — Mission, Vision, Philosophy, Laws, Definitions, Core Principles, Founding Principles, or Product DNA — not a product experiment, UX exploration, or temporary tactic.
2. **The Founder explicitly admits it** — Approve language is recorded (Decision Ledger or constitutional amendment).
3. **It is placed in the correct Canonical home** — Constitution, Forge Laws, or another Founder-designated Canonical instrument.
4. **Conflicts are resolved by amendment** — it does not silently overwrite an existing Article or Law.
5. **Working Knowledge alone never constitutes admission** — Idea Vault entries, research notes, and agent drafts remain Working until admitted.

**Effect of admission:** The statement becomes Canonical Knowledge. Changing it thereafter requires formal Founder approval under the Amendment Process.

**Effect of non-admission:** The statement may continue as Working Knowledge. It has no power to override this Constitution.

# Preamble

TalkForge exists so that people can walk into the conversations that shape their lives with greater clarity, courage, and capability.

Communication changes lives.

Every product decision should make someone more confident, more capable, and more connected through better communication.

# 1. Mission

## 1.1 Founding belief

> Nobody should ever feel voiceless because they don’t know how to express themselves.

## 1.2 Operational mission

TalkForge exists to help people **prepare for the conversations that shape their lives**.

Not to make people sound smarter.  
Not to generate perfect scripts.  
Not to keep people inside the app.

To help them walk into **real** conversations with greater clarity, courage, and capability.

## 1.3 Tagline (transformation)

> **Practice the conversations that shape your life.**

Every decision made by the company must strengthen this mission.

---

# 2. Vision

To become the world’s most trusted place to practice communication.

Success is measured by the quality of conversations users have **outside** the app — not by time spent inside it.

North star:

> Help people become someone who no longer avoids the conversations that matter.

Three-year feel:

> Welcome back. Last week you had that difficult conversation with your manager. I’ve been thinking about what happened. Ready to build on it?

---

# 3. Company Philosophy

TalkForge is a **Communication Gym**.

People do not come here to consume information. They come here to **practice**.

Great communicators are not born. They are built — through deliberate practice, coaching, reflection, and repetition.

Practice is **preparation**, never remediation for a “broken” communicator.

**AI’s role:** Coach, not replacement. Teach people to think — do not think for them. Encourage growth rather than dependency.

**Product decision filter (binding):**

> Forge should leave users feeling more understood than evaluated.

If a feature fails this test, it does not belong in TalkForge.

**Architecture founding sentence (binding):**

> TalkForge does not store information so it can remember the past. It organizes information so it can coach the future.

**Customer promise:**

> Every practice session should make the next real conversation easier.

Full philosophy counsel: `atlas/philosophy.md` (must remain aligned with this Constitution; on conflict, Constitution wins).

# 4. Core Principles

These principles bind every team, agent, and feature:

1. **Mission before features** — If it does not strengthen the mission, it is not built.
2. **Human growth over replacement** — Develop people; never remove their opportunity to think.
3. **Truth over invention** — Say when uncertain; ask when missing; never invent identity.
4. **Practice over theory** — Action over passive consumption.
5. **Dignity before performance** — Never trade dignity for metrics.
6. **Trust over growth hacks** — No manipulation, no addictive design, privacy respected.
7. **Simplicity with justification** — Complexity must earn its keep.
8. **Craftsmanship** — Speed never excuses poor quality.
9. **Continuity over restart** — A member should never have to introduce themselves twice.
10. **Understood over evaluated** — Emotional calibration before intelligence display.
11. **Evidence before Intelligence** — No identity claim without an explainable evidence chain.
12. **Purpose Autonomy** — Remember what matters; never decide what should matter.
13. **One Living Profile** — Single source of truth for who the member is becoming; experiences never write identity.
14. **Transfer is the scoreboard** — Real-life conversations matter more than time-in-app.
15. **Founder protects the mission** — Speed matters; mission alignment matters more.

# 9. Constitutional Articles

## Article I — Mission First

Every decision must support the mission.

If a feature does not help users become better communicators — and better prepared for the conversations that shape their lives — it should not be built.

---

## Article II — Human Growth

TalkForge exists to develop people, not replace them.

AI should coach, teach, challenge, and encourage.

AI should never remove the user’s opportunity to think.

---

## Article III — Truth

Atlas and every AI system must tell the truth.

If information is uncertain, it must say so.

If information is missing, it must ask for clarification instead of inventing answers.

---

## Article IV — Simplicity

Simple solutions are preferred over complicated ones.

Complexity must always justify itself.

---

## Article V — Practice Over Theory

People improve through practice.

Every feature should encourage action rather than passive consumption.

---

## Article VI — User Trust

User trust is more valuable than short-term growth.

Never manipulate users.

Never intentionally create addictive experiences.

Always respect privacy.

---

## Article VII — Quality

TalkForge is built with craftsmanship.

Features should feel polished, intentional, and useful.

Shipping quickly never excuses poor quality.

---

## Article VIII — Founder Responsibility

The Founder is responsible for protecting the mission.

Speed is important.

Mission alignment is more important.

---

## Article IX — Human Dignity

*(Adopted 2026-07-23 · AMD-001 · RES-017)*

TalkForge helps people discover, strengthen, and express the voice they already possess.

We do not fix broken people. We prepare people for conversations that matter.

Every interaction must protect and strengthen the user’s dignity, agency, and belief in their own ability to grow.

A user should never leave TalkForge feeling diminished.

If an experience improves performance while reducing dignity, it shall not ship.

Human dignity is never an acceptable tradeoff for engagement, retention, or optimization metrics.

Before approving any significant experience, apply the Human Dignity Test (AMD-001).

Full governing text: `atos/product/AMD-001-human-dignity-standard.md`.

---

## Article X — Purpose Autonomy

*(Adopted 2026-07-31 · Forge Law #015 · POM-001)*

Coach Forge may remember what matters to a member.

Coach Forge must never decide what should matter to a member.

Goals and Personal Principles are declared by the member — or not at all.

---

## Article XI — Evidence before Intelligence

*(Adopted 2026-07-31 · Forge Law #014 · POM-001)*

Forge shall never make a claim about a member that it cannot explain with evidence.

Insights without an observation chain shall not ship.

Transparency is part of product identity — not an optional UI flourish.

---

## Article XII — One Living Profile

*(Adopted 2026-07-31 · Forge Law #016 · LP-LAW-001 · POM-001)*

The Living Profile is the single source of truth for who the member is becoming.

Experiences never write identity.

TalkForge organizes information so it can coach the future — not merely so it can remember the past.

---

## Article XIII — Knowledge Planes

*(Adopted 2026-08-01 · EXEC-001 · Decision 048)*

TalkForge maintains two kinds of knowledge:

**Canonical Knowledge** — things that define the company (Mission, Constitution, Laws, Definitions, Core Philosophy). Formal approval is required to change them.

**Working Knowledge** — everything still being explored (product ideas, UX, research, experiments, future features, blind spots). Expected to evolve. Official system: Idea Vault.

Working Knowledge must not override Canonical Knowledge.

---

## Article XIV — Constitutional Admission

*(Adopted 2026-08-02 · Founder contingent Approve of EXEC-001 Step 2)*

Nothing that defines TalkForge is Canonical until it has been deliberately admitted by Founder decision (**Constitutional Law #001**).

Admission follows the **Constitutional Admission Rule**. Working Knowledge alone never amends this Constitution.

# 10. Decision Framework

Before approving any significant decision, ask:

1. Does this support the mission?
2. Does it improve communication / preparation for conversations that matter?
3. Does it increase user trust?
4. Is it simple?
5. Would we still build this five years from now?
6. Does this pass the Human Dignity Test (Article IX / AMD-001)?
7. Does this preserve Purpose Autonomy (Article X)?
8. Can every identity claim show evidence (Article XI)?
9. Does this respect one-way flow and Living Profile ownership (Article XII)?
10. Does this leave the user more understood than evaluated?
11. Does this respect Canonical vs Working knowledge planes (Article XIII)?
12. Does this respect Constitutional Law #001 and the Constitutional Admission Rule (Article XIV)?

If the answer to any of these is “No,” the decision requires further review.

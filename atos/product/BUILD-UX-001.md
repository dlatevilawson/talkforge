## Acceptance (Step 16)

1. First viewport of app home = one mission, not dashboard clutter.
2. Progress cannot own identity (LP-LAW).
3. Coach interaction passes understood-over-evaluated filter.

## Canonical ship filters (Decision 052)

1. **Craft Law #001** — more human, trustworthy, or courageous — never merely more intelligent (`CRAFT-LAW-001`).
2. **Design Principle #001** — reduce unnecessary cognitive load (`DES-001`).

Both must pass before ship.

## Experience authority (Decision 058 / CXA-001)

1. The CXA has design authority over member experience standards and may require redesign.
2. The Founder retains final production approval.
3. Homepage hierarchy is adaptive priority, not mandatory rendering.
4. Experience outcomes describe intended training objectives, never guaranteed personal transformation.
5. Progress metrics must not enter production before their operational definitions and evidence limitations are validated.
6. Beauty serves understanding; visually impressive work that delays comprehension or training fails.

All design proposals must include the twelve deliverables required by `CXA-001`.

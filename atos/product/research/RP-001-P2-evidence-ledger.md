## 0. Research rules (binding for Phase II+)

1. Do not assume any conclusion is true.  
2. Separate evidence from opinion.  
3. Distinguish correlation from causation.  
4. Identify conflicting research when it exists.  
5. Prefer systematic reviews, meta-analyses, foundational theory with cumulative support, and peer-reviewed research over individual opinions or cultural case vibes.  
6. Record uncertainty instead of filling gaps with assumptions.  
7. Every recommendation includes a confidence / evidence class.

### Evidence classes (ledger categories)

| Class | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Established Research** | Repeatedly supported by meta-analyses / cumulative theory programs; domain-appropriate |
| **Strong Evidence** | Solid empirical support; some boundary conditions or domain-transfer gaps remain |
| **Internal Evidence** | Repeatedly observed in TalkForge users / product / experiments (currently sparse) |
| **Working Hypothesis** | Plausible application to TalkForge; not yet sufficiently validated |
| **Open Question** | Unanswered; do not treat as institutional knowledge |

**Note:** Cultural story cases (Stream A) are **hypothesis generators**, not Established Research by themselves.

### P-001 — Autonomy support improves quality of motivation

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| **Principle** | Supporting autonomy (choice, volition) improves autonomous motivation, persistence, and well-being vs controlling pressure. |
| **Supporting evidence** | SDT meta-review of 60 meta-analyses (Ryan et al., 2022, *Psychological Bulletin*); need-support meta-analyses (e.g., Slemp et al., 2024; health-domain SDT metas). |
| **Contradicting / limits** | Autonomy is not absence of structure; some contexts need clear guidance. Effects vary by culture/domain. |
| **Class** | **Established Research** |
| **Ethical** | Pressure/guilt/FOMO CTAs conflict with this principle and AMD. |
| **TalkForge** | Dual CTAs, choiceful practice, no shame funnels — keep. |
| **Experiment** | A/B: controlling copy (“Don’t miss out”) vs autonomy-supportive (“Practice when you’re ready”) → waitlist quality + 7-day activation (not vanity clicks alone). |
| **Confidence for TF use** | Strong Evidence (application) |

### P-006 — Deliberate practice improves skills under specific conditions — but is not a complete explanation of expertise

| **Class** | **Strong Evidence** (helps training) / **conflict noted** on magnitude |
| **Ethical** | Do not promise “10,000 hours → mastery” or overnight transformation. |
| **TalkForge** | Gym metaphor OK if humble; measure transfer, not hours alone. |
| **Experiment** | Feedback-rich short reps vs unstructured free talk → rubric improvement + self-reported real-world use. |
| **Confidence for TF use** | Working Hypothesis for relationship transfer; Strong Evidence for in-simulator skill gains (to be shown) |

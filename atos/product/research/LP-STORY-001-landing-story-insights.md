## Eight research questions → LP checklist

| Question | LP application |
|---|---|
| 1. Emotional need? | Lead with **Competence + Respect + Connection** — not entertainment |
| 2. Engaging question? | Surface one curiosity gap near hero or belief: e.g. *What if the conversation that matters most was one you had already practiced?* |
| 3. Transformation? | Journey must show *who the user becomes* (someone prepared), not *what the app has* |
| 4. Peak moments? | Protect Belief block as emotional peak; ensure waitlist close is a calm second peak (invitation, not urgency theater) |
| 5. Memorable lines? | Limit new slogans; reinforce the two lines that already reframe identity |
| 6. Curiosity / tension / hope / empathy? | Curiosity = unanswered moment; tension = stakes of silence; hope = practice works; empathy = “never taught” |
| 7. Ethical principles? | No fake scarcity, no shame funnels, no trauma cosplay, no savior product |
| 8. Inspire without copying? | Steal **structure** (need→question→transformation→invitation), never scenes, slogans, or plot |

## Theme → concrete LP moves (do / don’t)

### Hope
- **Do:** Show transfer: practice → real conversation (already).  
- **Don’t:** Promise “master communication” as instant identity; “Master the Art…” is aspirational — balance with practice humility in subhead (already does).

### Belonging
- **Do:** Founding Members as cohort of practicers.  
- **Don’t:** Exclusive cool / genius club (anti–Think Different elitism).

### Courage
- **Do:** Name that hard talks include fear; practice is how courage gets reps.  
- **Don’t:** Heroic swagger; Nike energy without sweat-of-the-voice honesty.

### Love / Connection
- **Do:** Keep “Better relationships” as the why.  
- **Don’t:** Reduce love to conversion sentimentality.

### Redemption
- **Do:** Unpracticed ≠ unworthy (core).  
- **Don’t:** Before/after humiliation tropes.

### Identity
- **Do:** Invite “I practice for moments that matter.”  
- **Don’t:** Diagnose visitors (“anxious,” “awkward”) in public copy.

### Fear
- **Do:** Optional one-line normalization of nerves in Experience.  
- **Don’t:** Horror stories of ruined careers as fear ads.

### Growth
- **Do:** Keep staged journey.  
- **Don’t:** Feature grids in the first viewport (hero budget).

### Purpose
- **Do:** Tie CTAs to an upcoming real conversation.  
- **Don’t:** Abstract self-improvement fog.

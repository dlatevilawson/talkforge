## 1. Purpose

Study world-class examples across technology, psychology, education, storytelling, branding, product design, and business to identify **validated principles** TalkForge can ethically adapt, test, and improve.

**Standing rule:** Never copy features. Understand the principle → validate with evidence → improve in a mission-aligned way.

---

## 2. Scope

| In | Out |
|---|---|
| Principle extraction from exemplars | Feature cloning |
| Evidence-graded recommendations | Hype without sources |
| Adoption tests against mission/values | Parallel OS / new foundations without TEA §10 |

**Relationship:** BP-001 is the **exemplar-study method**. RP-001 / HEF-001 hold connection science; HBF holds behavior models. Do not duplicate those homes — cite them.

---

## 3. Core principles

1. Evidence before adoption.  
2. Mission and dignity before cleverness (AMD-001).  
3. Measurable user value required.  
4. Limits and failures documented as carefully as successes.  
5. One study → one case file; patterns promote only via evidence ledger / Institutional Approval.  

---

## 4. Operational process — Five-Step Analysis

### Step 1 — Problem & Appeal
- What problem does it solve?  
- Why do people value it?  
- Support with evidence (research, reviews, interviews, case studies, performance data).  

### Step 2 — Principles
Identify underlying principles across: Psychology · Design · Communication · Product · Business.

### Step 3 — Evidence Grade
Classify each significant claim:

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Established Research | Meta-analyses / cumulative science |
| Strong Evidence | Solid empirical support; limits remain |
| Industry Practice | Common in strong products; weaker causal proof |
| Internal Evidence | Observed in TalkForge |
| Working Hypothesis | Plausible; unvalidated here |
| Open Question | Unknown |

### Step 4 — Limits
Document: failure cases · audience limitations · hidden assumptions · contradictory evidence.

### Step 5 — Adoption Test
Eligible for adoption recommendation only if **all three are Yes**:

1. Supported by evidence?  
2. Aligns with TalkForge mission and values?  
3. Likely to create measurable value for users?  

If any answer is **No** → do not recommend adoption.

---

## 5. Decision criteria

| Result | Action |
|---|---|
| Passes Adoption Test | Draft applications + validation experiments |
| Fails evidence or mission | Archive as “studied, not adopted” with reason |
| Unclear value | Open Question + experiment — not institutional knowledge |

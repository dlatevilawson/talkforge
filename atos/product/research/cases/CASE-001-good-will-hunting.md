---

## 1. Human Need

**Primary need:** Belonging / Connection (to be known without performing)

**Secondary needs:** Respect · Identity · Love · Forgiveness (of self)

**HBF-001 mapping:** Connection + Respect (+ Competence as armor)

**Notes:** Genius is the costume; the need is safe attachment and dignity.

---

## 2. Emotional Journey

**Beginning →** Curiosity, admiration, distance (clever outsider)

**Middle →** Tension, protectiveness, ache (intimacy threatened)

**Ending →** Relief, hope, tenderness (risk toward love)

**Dominant arc label:** Armor → openness

---

## 3. Curiosity Engine

**Core question:** Can someone who is brilliant also allow himself to be loved — without running?

**How the work delays the answer:** Deflection, intellect-as-shield, failed intimacy, mentor pressure

**When the question resolves (or deliberately doesn’t):** Resolves in action (leaving for relationship/risk), not a speech alone

---

## 4. Character Transformation

**Who is the character at the beginning?**  
Orphaned prodigy using intellect and aggression to avoid being known.

↓

**What changes?**  
Safe mirroring from a mentor; repeated removal of false self-blame; choice to risk connection.

↓

**Who do they become?**  
Someone willing to be seen — competence no longer required as worth.

**Is transformation earned through agency, grace, or both?** Both — grace (mentor) unlocks agency (choice to go).

## 8. Psychological Principles

**About needs:** People often defend Connection with Competence performance.

**About fear / avoidance:** Avoidance can look like strength (wit, anger, exit).

**About identity / belonging:** Shame says “I am the damage”; healing separates event from self-worth.

**About change:** Insight is insufficient; change is a behavioral risk toward belonging.

**Evidence class for these claims:** Strong Evidence (attachment/shame/self-efficacy literatures) · Working Hypothesis (TalkForge session design mapping)

## 10. Ethical Check

**Risk:** Mixed

**If yes — manipulation path:** Using “it’s not your fault”–style emotional intensity to create dependency on the product as therapist.

**Ethical use constraint (AMD-001):** TalkForge is a practice gym, not clinical therapy; no trauma mining for conversion; no claiming to heal wounds.

**Forbidden use:** Shame-then-save funnels; fake intimacy; pathologizing users in copy or coach voice.

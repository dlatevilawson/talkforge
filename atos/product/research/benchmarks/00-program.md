## 1. Objective

Build the **benchmark database** that guides TalkForge decisions.

| Old question | New question |
|---|---|
| What should we build? | **Who has already mastered this?** |

Everything else — product features, branding, onboarding, AI coaching — builds on this foundation instead of intuition.

## 3. Method (Phases 2–3)

### Phase 2 — Find the leaders

For each domain, identify with **justification** (not opinion):

- Top researchers  
- Landmark books  
- Seminal papers  
- Leading organizations  
- Products recognized as best-in-class (when applicable)  
- Why they are considered leaders  
- Quality of supporting evidence  

### Phase 3 — Knowledge map (per benchmark)

Each benchmark answers:

1. What problem do they solve?  
2. What principle makes them successful?  
3. What evidence supports it?  
4. What are the limitations?  
5. Should TalkForge **adopt**, **adapt**, or **reject** it?  

Adoption decisions use the [BP-001 Adoption Test](../connection-science/06-best-practices-method.md): evidence × mission/dignity × measurable user value.

### Evidence grades (aligned with BP-001 / RP-001-P2)

| Grade | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Established Research | Meta-analyses / cumulative science |
| Strong Evidence | Solid empirical support; limits remain |
| Industry Practice | Common among strong orgs/products; weaker causal proof |
| Working Hypothesis | Plausible for TalkForge; unvalidated here |
| Open Question | Unknown — do not treat as knowledge |

1. Research the **field**, not tip lists.  
2. Separate evidence from opinion.  
3. Record conflicts and revisions.  
4. Cross-reference existing volumes; do not duplicate Connection Science ledgers.

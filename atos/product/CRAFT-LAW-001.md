## Law

> **Every implementation should make TalkForge feel more human, more trustworthy, or more courageous—never merely more intelligent.**

---

## Emotional pillars

| Feel | Meaning | Architectural expression |
|---|---|---|
| **Understood** | Human | Living Profile · Coach calibration (Forge Law #013) |
| **Safe to practice** | Trustworthy | Evidence · Provenance · Dignity · Continuity |
| **Ready to act** | Courageous | Readiness · Coach · Transfer outside the app |

At least one pillar must strengthen. Intelligence display without these pillars shall not ship.

---

## Implementation test

Before shipping any implementation, ask:

1. Does this make TalkForge feel more **human**, more **trustworthy**, or more **courageous**?
2. Or does it merely make TalkForge seem more **intelligent**?

If (2) without (1) — redesign or reject.

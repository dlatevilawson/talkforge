## What could fail?

The fixed topic, audience, pattern, or urgency catalogs may force a visitor into categories that do not fit, imply that deterministic “Diagnose” output is a clinical or System 1 inference, or make a provisional guest draft look like settled identity. Authentication could also become a dead end if the selected path is lost, or implementation could add a second confirmation screen that recreates the friction the pivot removes.

## Unintended consequences

- Topic cards narrow the member’s intent too early or privilege workplace scenarios.
- “Something else” custom text is hidden, unbounded, or incorrectly offered for every topic/context question.
- Multi-select cardinality drifts from topics 1–3 or audiences multi-select; pattern/urgency drift from single-select.
- “Diagnose” output invents meaning beyond the selected catalogs/templates.
- Anonymous selections are treated as durable Living Profile truth before authentication.
- `member_practice_profile` becomes a parallel profile or activation silently overwrites richer member-declared values.
- Teams retain conversational discovery, semantic value, or turn-cap logic beside the wizard and create two competing FTUEs.
- Authentication succeeds but the handoff is lost, producing a cold Forge session.

## Trust impact

High if TalkForge appears to decide who the member is, loses selections at authentication, or asks the same questions again after signup.

## Autonomy impact

High unless “Something else” provides bounded custom text, **Adjust** returns to Phase 2 prefilled, and member-declared values take precedence over inference.

## Complexity impact

Medium. The deterministic path should delete branching and semantic-gate concepts from the active architecture; retaining both systems creates avoidable state, analytics, and recovery complexity.

## Mission alignment

Aligned when the wizard quickly prepares a real conversation and sends an authenticated member into contextual practice. Misaligned if the cards become a personality quiz, diagnosis, or engagement funnel.

## Mitigation / decision

Decision 060 supersedes the Decision 059 conversational discovery/value-gate design while preserving Decision 059 as historical record. Enforce the exact Phase 1 topic catalog and 1–3 cardinality; exact Phase 2 audience/pattern/urgency catalogs and selection modes; and deterministic Phase 3 verification contract. Only “Something else” may reveal bounded custom text. Store provisional guest values only in the signed server-side anonymous session for up to 14 days. After **Looks right**, require authentication for guests, then persist declarations in the one authorized Living Profile `member_practice_profile` JSONB field with member provenance and verification timestamps. This field is not a parallel profile and does not contain System 1 inference. Make activation idempotent and route directly to contextual Forge with no duplicate post-auth confirmation. Keep Assessment reachable but demoted. Test catalog IDs/labels, cardinality, prefilled Adjust, deterministic template output, auth retry, merge precedence, and unauthenticated Forge denial.

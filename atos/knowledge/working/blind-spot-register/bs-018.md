## What could fail?

Forge Agent sends check-ins the member did not ask for, infers upcoming conversations from history, treats check-in copy as identity, or keeps pinging after decline. Outreach becomes habit anxiety (IV-REJ-001) or an experience that writes who the member is becoming (IV-REJ-003).

## Unintended consequences

- Members mute TalkForge or lose trust that Forge only speaks when invited.
- Invented “events” become fake commitments and leak into Living Profile.
- Deny/dismiss is ignored; the queue re-materializes the same nag.
- Live VoiceArena or guest preview is overloaded to deliver outreach.

## Trust impact

High — unsolicited coaching feels extractive.

## Autonomy impact

High if the product decides what conversations matter without a member declaration (Forge Law #015).

## Complexity impact

Medium if calendar, email, or auto-approval land before the approval inbox is proven.

## Mission alignment

Preparation is invited. Notifications support a declared conversation; they do not manufacture a habit loop.

## Mitigation / decision

Mitigating 2026-09-11 (IV-PROD-011 / Decision 061, Phases 0–2):

- Opt-in default OFF.
- Cues are member-declared only; no calendar; no inferred events; no homework auto-cues.
- Every check-in states why it was sent; Approve / Deny is required; deny stops that cue class.
- In-app only. No identity writes. No VoiceArena / Realtime / guest preview changes.
- Cron and LLM drafting wait for a later go-ahead.

Mitigating 2026-09-12 (Decision 062, Phase 3 Stage A — not shipped until Codex apply + smoke):

- Cron drafts stay pending_approval. No auto-approval. No sendNotification.
- Paid generation is reserved atomically (one draft_attempt per member per UTC day).
- Stale drafting rows are recovered with template copy so lazy inbox cannot stay blocked.
- Living Profile is read-only via buildCoachContext. No Coach memory or identity writes.

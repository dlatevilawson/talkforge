# BS-014 — Coach excellence becomes theater without conversation evidence

## What could fail?

We ship prompt language that *describes* world-class coaching while Forge still behaves like a questionnaire, advice engine, or rigid script. Reviewers then treat technical green checks (mic, STT, persistence) as readiness and skip evidence from real conversations.

## Unintended consequences

- Members feel evaluated or interviewed instead of coached.
- Practice ratio inverts (Forge talks more than the member).
- First-time experience becomes onboarding interrogation instead of discovery.
- Marketing promise (“communication excellence”) fails the dual ship test (Craft Law #001 + DES-001).

## Trust impact

High — trust is earned through the quality of Forge’s communication, not feature count.

## Autonomy impact

Medium — over-coaching and rigid scripts reduce member agency.

## Complexity impact

Low for encoding standards; high if we add scoring UI or dashboards that substitute for listening.

## Mission alignment

Forge must demonstrate the skills TalkForge teaches. Listening, judgment, and deliberate practice beat polished explanations.

## Mitigation / decision

Mitigating 2026-08-06: encode CFX-001 into `FORGE_MENTOR_PHILOSOPHY`, voice openings, and session wrap rules; Home → Training Room passes a purposeful starting place that Forge holds lightly; release gate requires evidence from real conversations (IV-AI-006). Residual: human review of live sessions remains mandatory before claiming readiness.

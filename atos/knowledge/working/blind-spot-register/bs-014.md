## What could fail?

Discover-first FTUE showcases seven Machines to build curiosity. If Continuity Home or post-training default routes become the same equal Machine grid, TalkForge reintroduces mission-menu home (IV-REJ-005 / AUDIT-001 C3) and decision fatigue.

## Unintended consequences

- Members never form a single continuity next step.
- “Explore” becomes the product instead of coaching.
- Living Profile / readiness chain is skipped permanently.

## Trust impact

Medium–High. Exciting discovery that never narrows feels like a content catalog, not a coach.

## Autonomy impact

Medium. Free explore is good; endless equal choice without recommendation undermines Forge.

## Complexity impact

Medium. Two surfaces (Discover vs Home) must stay distinct in code and copy.

## Mission alignment

Aligned if Discover is **first-run only** (or explicit browse) and Home stays one recommendation. Misaligned if Machines grid replaces Continuity Home.

## Mitigation / decision

Watching 2026-08-05: Discover lives on `/onboarding` until complete; Continuity Home remains single-CTA. Do not mount TrainingFocusPicker / DiscoverGym as `/app` home.

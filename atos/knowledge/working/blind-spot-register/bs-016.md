## What could fail?

Membership UX pressures upgrades (dark patterns), interrupts live coaching when a limit hits, locks members out of their account, or invents a parallel identity/analytics store that violates OWN-001 / FREEZE-001. Client-trusted subscription flags could grant Pro without Stripe verification.

## Unintended consequences

- Trust collapse: product feels extractive instead of preparatory.
- Members lose access mid-session or to history/profile.
- Refunds/chargebacks from unclear status messaging (past_due, canceled).
- Plans surface becomes a mission menu (IV-REJ-005).

## Trust impact

High — money + coaching intimacy.

## Autonomy impact

High if practice is cut off mid-session or account locked.

## Complexity impact

Medium — Stripe webhooks + entitlement gates; keep Free/Pro only to bound scope.

## Mission alignment

Subscriptions must be earned by value (“I want to keep practicing”), never pressure.

## Mitigation / decision

Mitigating 2026-08-07 (IV-PROD-008 / BILL-001):
- Server-side Stripe webhooks as SSOT; never trust client plan flags.
- Configurable free limits; never interrupt an in-progress session.
- End-of-free uses calm Forge copy + Continue with Pro / Maybe Later; app exploration stays open.
- Membership page (not Pricing); Stripe Customer Portal for manage/cancel/invoices.
- Sync only `user` ↔ `premium` role; never rewrite Living Profile identity.
- Past_due keeps access during Smart Retries.

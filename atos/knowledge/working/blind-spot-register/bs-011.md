## What could fail?

`ensurePersistedLivingProfile` always writes a `display_name` (from account profile, metadata, email local-part, or `"Member"`). `evaluateReadiness` treats **any** of name / purpose / principle / season as `profileGatePassed`. After ensure, **Begin and `/app/practice` unlock for essentially every authenticated member**, even when purpose, principles, and seasons are empty.

The Begin ↔ practice persistence mismatch is fixed. The dependency-chain intent (Living Profile context → readiness → one recommendation → coaching) can still be skipped at the quality layer.

## Unintended consequences

- Homepage “Set your training focus” becomes rare or dead for normal signups.
- Coach Forge opens with almost no declared context; coaching quality and trust drop.
- Side links (`/app/dashboard`, progress, MissionPicker) bounce less often, so the soft “complete LP first” UX disappears.
- Teams may treat “practice reachable” as “readiness working.”

## Trust impact

High. Members enter a Training Room that feels generic or unprepared; Coach appears not to know them.

## Autonomy impact

Medium. Skipping focus-setting removes a deliberate orientation step; “Not today” / redirect still exist, but the recommended path no longer requires meaning.

## Complexity impact

Low technically; high product risk if left unexamined.

## Mission alignment

Conflicts with Living Profile → Readiness → Adaptive Homepage → Coaching unless Founder accepts name-only as sufficient V1 readiness.

## Mitigation / decision

2026-08-05 (earlier): required purpose/principle/season before Begin.

**Founder override 2026-08-05 (IV-UX-009):** training focus is **optional**. Persisted Living Profile unlocks Begin; visual Machines picker + Skip replace the questionnaire. Coaching quality risk accepted in favor of frictionless entry; optional focus remains available from onboarding and `/app/focus`.

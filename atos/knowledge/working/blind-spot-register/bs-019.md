## What could fail?

The baseline card could make signal labels look like measured results; an incomplete assessment could lose answers or appear complete; old profile text could be presented as current; a check-in or voluntary tune could rewrite an existing member's generated plan; or the new onboarding tap could leak into the Decision 060 Coach preview as a form gate.

## Unintended consequences

- Members mistake “Not measured yet” or legacy presence scores for current readiness.
- A tab close loses diagnostic progress or the assessment restarts at the first slot.
- Member-declared corrections overwrite a generated identity claim without provenance.
- Existing plan days or history shift after a preference edit.
- The Coach preview becomes a wizard and violates its post-session auth boundary.

## Trust impact

High — a false baseline or lost answer makes the product feel like it is grading or forgetting the member.

## Autonomy impact

High if the system chooses a bottleneck, daily dose, or plan change without member confirmation.

## Complexity impact

Medium — migration, assessment persistence/resume, and UI state must remain consistent.

## Mission alignment

The product should help the member rehearse with a clear next step; it must not make unsupported claims about readiness or turn a voluntary preference into a forced commitment.

## Mitigation / decision

- Nullable `daily_minutes`; no backfill/default at database level. Validate only 5/10/15; blank remains null.
- Reuse the existing seven slots. Persist partial accepted answers through the existing assessment completion route and resume from server-stored provenance.
- Incomplete profile gets only “We're still learning your starting point.” The five signals remain “Not measured yet” absent current-session evidence. Do not expose legacy presence scores in this card.
- Keep member-declared bottleneck corrections provenance-backed. Check-in must be explicit; tuning never changes current plan days, blocks, or history.
- Decision 060 placement check: settings remain on post-auth onboarding, not the `/coach` track.
- No production migration apply or data backfill is part of this code build.

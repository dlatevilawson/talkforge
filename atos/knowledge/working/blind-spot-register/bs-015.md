# BS-015 — First-session rating becomes a survey or fires at the wrong time

## What could fail?

The check-in appears every session, before the wrap is ready, before save succeeds, or as a long form — turning a premium ending into homework and violating DES-001.

## Unintended consequences

- Members feel evaluated after their first practice (conflicts with Forge Law #013 / understood over evaluated).
- Incomplete sessions or failed saves still prompt → bad data and broken trust.
- Rating on the optional Reflect page is skipped by “Done for now,” so first-session signal is lost.

## Trust impact

Medium — timing and tone decide whether the ending feels thoughtful or extractive.

## Autonomy impact

Low if dismissible; medium if forced multi-step forms.

## Complexity impact

Low when scoped to one bottom sheet + one follow-up + once-only persistence.

## Mission alignment

Purpose is understanding first impression while ending thoughtfully — not maximizing survey completion.

## Mitigation / decision

Mitigating 2026-08-06: show only after first-session wrap + successful save; gate on pre-complete `!isReturning`; unique `user_id` row; local once-cache; mission question → stars → one option → optional note → thank-you; always Skip; internal retention signals separate from the prompt. Residual: production migration must be applied for durable storage.

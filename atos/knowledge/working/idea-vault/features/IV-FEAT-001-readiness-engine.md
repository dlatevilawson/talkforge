## Statement

Versioned, evidence-linked readiness measurement for Purpose, Perspective,
Composure, Message, and Adaptability. Readiness is qualitative and
self-referenced: no 0–100 score, no cross-member comparison, and no guaranteed
real-world outcome. It becomes the measurement foundation for the later
Assess → Prescribe → Train → Re-assess loop.

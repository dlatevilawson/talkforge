## Statement

TalkForge has two brains: **Coach Brain** (inside the product — listening, cadence, influence) and **Marketing Brain** (at the front door — why humans stop, click, trust, and begin). Marketing never sells software, AI, or tools. It invites people to prepare for high-stakes real-life conversations. Golden rule: nobody wakes up wanting an AI communication simulator; they wake up hoping they don’t mess up today’s presentation.

---

## Why it matters

Growth fails when the front door speaks engineering. Alignment from ad → landing → onboarding → session copy is required so attention converts into first practice.

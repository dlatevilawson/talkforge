## Statement

A Growth-plane agent that **drafts** ads and campaign plans from the Marketing Brain, then presents them to the Founder. It does **not** spend money, publish, or change bids unless the Founder approves that specific action. It speaks the conversation the human is about to have — never the technology. Scoreboard is founding members who begin practice, not clicks or time-in-app.

---

## Why it matters

TalkForge already has a Marketing Brain (doctrine) and no operator that turns it into campaigns. Founder asked whether we can run ads and create campaigns. Capture this before build talk. An unsupervised spender would violate Atlas’s notify-not-fix posture, Craft Law #001, and the rejected engagement metric.

## Notes

Working Knowledge only. Not Canonical. Not a ship authorization.

**Do not build now.** AUDIT-001.2 / Decision 053: **NO-GO** for feature expansion except the Assistant Coach track (Decision 059). A campaign agent is unrelated feature expansion.

If later approved, hard gates:

1. **Draft, don’t spend.** Founder approves each publish, budget, and audience. Atlas notifies; this agent does not “fix” growth by buying traffic.
2. **MKT-001 voice.** Blacklist in ads: AI · Machine · Analysis · Simulator · Voice Model · Assessment · Engine. Sell the conversation, not the product.
3. **IV-REJ-001.** Do not optimize for clicks, impressions, or time-in-app. Prefer founding-member begin / first practice.
4. **Separate from Coach and Forge.** This agent never writes Living Profile or coaches members.
5. **Credentials.** Meta / Google / other ad APIs stay Founder-owned secrets. No silent account access.
6. **Honest claims.** Craft Law #001 — no unearned “unshakeable” promises the product cannot keep.

Safe first slice (only after Founder Decision + roadmap): generate 3–5 ad variants + one landing angle from MKT-001, show them in FounderOS, require explicit approve. No API spend in v1.

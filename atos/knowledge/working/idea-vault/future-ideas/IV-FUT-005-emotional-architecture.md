## Statement

Establish Emotional Architecture as a first-class TalkForge discipline for deliberately shaping anticipation, safety, courage, focus, relief, pride, motivation, and memory across the Communication Gym. Explore whether this discipline requires a future Chief Emotion Architect role or should remain a specialized authority within the Chief Experience Architect organization.

---

## Why it matters

Usability alone cannot make TalkForge memorable. Members arrive before difficult real-world conversations and need experiences that protect dignity, create psychological safety, support honest practice, and leave them feeling more capable. Emotional intent should therefore be specified and tested with the same rigor as interaction, accessibility, and cognitive load.

## Notes

- CXA-001 currently assigns Emotional Design and the complete human experience to the CXA. A separate peer executive would require explicit amendment of that Canonical authority model.
- Recommended structure for exploration: Emotional Architecture as a first-class CXA discipline, standard, and review lens before creating another executive office.
- A future Chief Emotion Architect could become a specialist reporting within the CXA organization if product scale justifies dedicated leadership.
- Emotional design must never manipulate retention, manufacture urgency, punish decline, or substitute celebration for real preparedness.
- Phase 9 should not bypass Phase 8 implementation, testing, or certification.
- “Today, we’re training this” remains proposed brand language until explicitly admitted.

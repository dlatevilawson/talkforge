## Statement

“Set your training focus” is optional. Instead of a long questionnaire, members pick (or skip) a visual Machines card that names one training focus. Begin today’s training stays primary on Continuity Home; the picker is an onboarding / refine surface — never a mission-menu home.

---

## Why it matters

Founder directive: onboard frictionless and exciting. Forced purpose text blocked Begin and felt like homework. A visual Machines selection preserves optional Living Profile context without restoring equal mission tiles as home (IV-REJ-005 / AUDIT-001 C3).

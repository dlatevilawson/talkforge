## Statement

Create a universal behavioral language governing how TalkForge responds, moves, waits, listens, recovers, illuminates, sounds, and confirms across every environment and Machine. Behavior becomes a recognizable part of the Communication Gym identity without changing the information architecture established by CXA-001.

---

## Why it matters

Visual consistency alone cannot make TalkForge feel like one connected training facility. Consistent timing, causality, emotional pacing, recovery, sound, lighting, and haptics can reduce hesitation and strengthen confidence—but inconsistent or ornamental behavior can make the product feel artificial, manipulative, or unreliable.

## Notes

- The Chief Interaction Architect is a specialist role under CXA accountability and does not alter the admitted authority model.
- MBL-001 operationalizes CXA-001 and TDS-001; it does not redesign their information architecture.
- Behavior remains fully understandable without color, motion, sound, or haptics.
- Sound and haptics are optional enhancements, never required channels.
- Anti-manipulation rules prohibit attention capture, variable rewards, false urgency, and punitive behavior.
- UI implementation and release gates remain unchanged.

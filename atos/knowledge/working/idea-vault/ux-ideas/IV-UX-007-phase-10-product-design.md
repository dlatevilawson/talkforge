## Statement

Transform CXA-001 and TDS-001 into production-ready high-fidelity product designs before UI implementation. Phase 10 covers Coach Homepage, Gym Floor, Training Room, Reflection Room, Progress Center, Profile, and Settings across responsive layouts, interaction states, recovery states, and accessibility requirements.

---

## Why it matters

Engineering should translate an approved experience blueprint rather than invent hierarchy, emotion, and interaction while coding. High-fidelity design makes intent testable before implementation and exposes cognitive-load, trust, accessibility, and cross-environment continuity problems while changes remain inexpensive.

## Notes

- Founder gate: no React, Tailwind, or product implementation during this design phase.
- Every screen must state its purpose, emotional objective, supported decision, removed cognitive load, and Communication Gym role.
- The Chief Visual Design Architect is a specialist discipline under CXA accountability unless the Founder explicitly amends the authority model.
- Figma integration was unavailable during the initial exploration because the configured MCP server required authentication. Initial deliverables are portable vector design boards, not a substitute for the final componentized Figma source.

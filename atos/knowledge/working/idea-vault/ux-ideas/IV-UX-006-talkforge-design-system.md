## Statement

Create a unified design system for the Communication Gym before Phase 8 UI implementation. The system defines how TalkForge feels across Coach, Gym Floor, Training Room, Reflection Room, Progress Center, Profile, Settings, and future Machines while preserving one coherent experience language.

---

## Why it matters

TalkForge already has strong experience law and an official brand mark, but its marketing, application, authentication, and training surfaces use fragmented typography, color, motion, components, and state patterns. A shared system makes beauty serve understanding, prevents arbitrary UI invention, and allows the Gym to expand without losing identity.

## Notes

- The official Founder mark and gold values remain protected.
- “Beauty Has a Job” is proposed as a CXA amendment; it is not Canonical until Founder admission.
- Progress percentages and comparative claims remain prohibited until operational definitions and evidence models exist.

## Statement

Atlas helps by **learning and understanding** what is going on across TalkForge — head to toe. Atlas must know every material part of the company (Canonical doctrine, Working Knowledge, product systems, live failure modes, and what is not in place). When something is off balance, the Founder should learn it **without having to ask**. Atlas is **not** in charge of fixing; engineering owns remediation. Atlas’s job is to be **the first to know** and to keep the Founder in touch with every failure and every gap that matters.

---

## Why it matters

Today Atlas is largely frozen as institutional stewardship (docs, gates, observation with Founder-visible delivery off). That leaves product failures (missing Forge intro, Arena join glitches, assessment question loops) invisible until the Founder notices or an engineer is tasked. Without a company-wide awareness loop, TalkForge can ship while Atlas stays quiet — the opposite of stewardship.

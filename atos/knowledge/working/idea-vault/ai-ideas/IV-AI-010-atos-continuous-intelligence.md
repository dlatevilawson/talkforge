## Statement

TalkForge’s next architectural milestone is **ATOS Continuous Intelligence**: unify Executive Memory, event ingestion, Working Knowledge, agent coordination, outcome learning, Internal Company Awareness, and External Technology Intelligence around the six existing ATOS specifications (SPEC-001–006). Destination is a governed autonomous company-learning loop: company and external events → Context → Executive Memory → Atlas reasoning → plans/delegation → agent or human execution → outcomes → learning → Memory Keeper. No seventh ATOS system. SPECs stay frozen. Atlas remains non-sovereign.

---

## Why it matters

Atlas today can counsel from institutional files and notice some internal ops failures, then forget a sitting unless the browser still holds it. That is not company learning. Continuous Intelligence is how Atlas becomes first to know, able to remember what must persist, and eventually able to execute only inside delegated authority — without silently amending Identity, Constitution, Laws, frozen SPECs, or Canonical truth.

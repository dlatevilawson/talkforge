## Statement

Passing technical tests does not mean Forge is ready. Forge is only ready when it consistently communicates with the judgment, adaptability, emotional intelligence, and teaching ability of an exceptional communication coach. Every release claim about Coach Forge must be supported by evidence from real conversations, reviewed against the Communication Excellence Assessment (CFX-001).

---

## Why it matters

TalkForge promises communication mastery. If Forge itself sounds like a scripted chatbot, questionnaire, or advice engine, the product contradicts its mission. Technical readiness (mic, STT, session state) is necessary but never sufficient.

## Context

TalkForge had growing doctrine (SYS1/SYS2, laws, CE) spread across chats and PRs. Risk of losing philosophy and shipping misaligned features.

## Problem

How do we ensure nothing valuable is lost and every future decision stays mission-aligned?

## Decision

Adopt EXEC-001: Foundation → Strategy → Execution. Start with Idea Vault, then Constitution. Gate each step on Founder review (later continuum authorized for Steps 3–18).

## Reasoning

Building features without a knowledge OS recreates the giant-notebook / silent-Canonical-drift failure. Capture, connect, admit, then build.

## Lessons Learned

- Working vs Canonical must be explicit (Article XIII, Law #001).
- Vault schema temptation is real — freeze early (Step 1).
- Contingent Approve works when the missing instruments are named.

## Future Reflection

Revisit whether Phase 3 execution speed is constrained too hard by documentation — or whether documentation is what enables speed.
